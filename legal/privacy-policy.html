By accessing or using XPARQ, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Users under 13 years of age are prohibited from creating an account or using the platform.

During registration, XPARQ may require age verification through date-of-birth information. Accounts determined to belong to individuals under 13 years old may be denied access or removed.

5. Messaging and Encryption

XPARQ provides encrypted messaging services.

Private chat messages are encrypted on your device before they are transmitted or stored. Message bodies are kept in encrypted form on our servers. XPARQ does not hold the private keys needed to decrypt those protected messages under normal operation.

Limited metadata (such as delivery time, participants, and message type) may still be processed to operate the service. Optional notification previews, if enabled in settings, may show message content on your device. Quoted-reply snippets in metadata are also stored in encrypted form.

Group chats use separate encrypted envelopes for each member (pairwise sessions). Decrypted message caches on your device are protected with keys stored in the device secure enclave.

If secure messaging components are unavailable on a device, sending may be blocked rather than sending readable text to our servers.

6. Voice and Video Communications

Voice and video communications are protected in transit using industry-standard encryption technologies.

While communications are secured against unauthorized interception during transmission, end-to-end encryption may not be available for all media communication modes.

XPARQ does not record voice calls or video calls as part of normal platform operations.

8. Safety Systems

To maintain a safe environment, XPARQ provides:

  • Reporting tools
  • Blocking tools
  • Mute controls

  • Content moderation systems
  • Abuse prevention measures

Users may report content or accounts that violate platform rules.

8.1 Age Verification for Adult Content (18+)

Optional identity verification is required to access the Black Hole Zone (age-restricted, 18+ content areas). XPARQ uses Stripe Identity to verify that you are at least 18 years old.

  • Verification is voluntary and only requested when you choose to enable adult-content features.
  • During verification you may be asked to provide government-issued identification and a live selfie. These are collected and processed by Stripe, not stored as readable ID images on XPARQ servers.
  • XPARQ stores only the outcome on your account profile: whether verification succeeded, when it was completed, and a reference to the verification session (not your ID images).
  • Stripe's Privacy Policy applies to data submitted during Identity verification: https://stripe.com/privacy
  • You may contact Stripe or XPARQ to exercise rights related to verification data according to applicable law and each provider's policies.

8.2 Automated Image Moderation

Before certain images are published, XPARQ may send the image to Sightengine (a third-party moderation provider) via our secure backend for automated safety scoring (e.g. nudity detection). Images are transmitted for analysis only. XPARQ receives a safety classification and may block uploads or apply age-restricted labels when thresholds are met. Sightengine's privacy policy: https://sightengine.com/privacy

XPARQ does not generally access, inspect, store, or process prompts submitted through user-supplied third-party AI credentials.

Use of third-party AI services remains subject to the privacy policies and terms of those providers.
