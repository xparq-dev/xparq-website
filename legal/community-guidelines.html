XPARQ Community Guidelines

Last Updated: 3 June 2026

Welcome to XPARQ

XPARQ is built to connect people, communities, ideas, and technology in a safe and respectful environment.

These Community Guidelines explain what is allowed, what is prohibited, and how we work together to maintain a healthy ecosystem for everyone.

By using XPARQ, you agree to follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Other People

Treat others with respect.

Healthy debate, discussion, and disagreement are welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, and abuse are not.

Allowed:

  • Constructive criticism
  • Civil disagreement
  • Respectful discussion
  • Honest feedback

Not Allowed:

  • Bullying
  • Threats
  • Targeted harassment
  • Doxxing
  • Coordinated attacks
  • Hate campaigns

2. No Hate Speech

XPARQ does not allow content that promotes hatred, violence, or discrimination against individuals or groups.

This includes attacks based on:

  • Race
  • Ethnicity
  • Nationality
  • Religion
  • Disability
  • Gender
  • Sexual orientation
  • Age
  • Other protected characteristics

Content intended to humiliate, dehumanize, or incite harm against others may be removed.

3. Protect Children and Young Users

Safety of minors is a top priority.

Users under the age of 13 are not permitted to use XPARQ.

Content involving:

  • Child exploitation
  • Grooming
  • Sexualization of minors
  • Child abuse material

is strictly prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required by law.

Users aged 13–17 may be subject to additional safety protections and content restrictions.

4. Adult Content

XPARQ may support restricted adult-content areas for eligible adult users.

Adult content must:

  • Remain legal
  • Be appropriately labeled
  • Follow platform restrictions
  • Respect consent and privacy

Users under 18 years of age may not access adult-content features.

Non-consensual intimate content is prohibited.

Content involving minors is strictly prohibited.

5. Violence and Dangerous Activities

Do not post content that:

  • Encourages violence
  • Promotes terrorism
  • Glorifies violent crime
  • Incites harm against others

Educational, journalistic, historical, or public-interest discussions may be permitted when appropriately contextualized.

6. Illegal Activities

Do not use XPARQ to facilitate, organize, promote, or advertise illegal activities.

Examples include:

  • Fraud
  • Identity theft
  • Human trafficking
  • Distribution of illegal materials
  • Financial scams
  • Criminal activity

Illegal activity may result in immediate account suspension.

7. Privacy and Personal Information

Respect the privacy of others.

Do not share:

  • Private addresses
  • Government identification documents
  • Personal phone numbers without consent
  • Private communications without authorization
  • Sensitive personal information belonging to others

Users should control their own privacy settings and use available safety features such as Ghost Mode, Block, and Report tools.

8. Spam and Manipulation

Do not:

  • Send spam
  • Mass-message users
  • Artificially inflate engagement
  • Create fake accounts
  • Manipulate platform systems

Automation intended to abuse platform functionality may result in enforcement action.

9. Intellectual Property

Only share content that you have permission to use.

Do not upload or distribute:

  • Copyrighted content without authorization
  • Pirated software
  • Unauthorized media
  • Stolen intellectual property

Repeated violations may lead to account restrictions.

10. AI-Generated Content

AI-generated content is permitted.

However:

  • Users remain responsible for content they publish
  • AI output must not be used to deceive, scam, impersonate, or harass others
  • AI-generated misinformation may be removed if it creates a significant risk of harm

Disclosure of AI assistance is encouraged where appropriate.

11. Communities and Group Spaces

Community owners, moderators, and administrators are expected to maintain safe environments.

Additional community rules may be established, provided they do not conflict with XPARQ policies.

XPARQ may intervene when community management fails to address serious violations.

12. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, use the in-app reporting tools.

Reports may include:

  • User reports
  • Content reports
  • Harassment reports
  • Spam reports
  • Safety concerns

Reports are reviewed through a combination of automated systems and human evaluation processes.

13. Enforcement Actions

When violations occur, XPARQ may:

  • Remove content
  • Reduce visibility
  • Restrict features
  • Issue warnings
  • Suspend accounts
  • Permanently terminate accounts

Enforcement decisions are based on the severity, frequency, and impact of violations.

Repeated violations may result in permanent loss of platform access.

14. Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was taken incorrectly, you may contact XPARQ and request a review.

Submission of an appeal does not guarantee reversal of a decision.

XPARQ reserves the right to make final moderation decisions.

15. Building the Galaxy Together

XPARQ exists to support communication, collaboration, creativity, and technological innovation.

Every user contributes to the health of the ecosystem.

Help us keep the galaxy safe, respectful, and welcoming for everyone.
